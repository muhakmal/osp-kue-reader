### Short Description
Instantly check e-Money, Flazz, and TapCash balances & history via NFC!

### Full Description
**PembacaKUE** is a smart utility app that transforms your NFC-enabled smartphone into a versatile electronic money (e-money) card reader. Say goodbye to waiting in line at ATMs or convenience stores just to check the balance of your toll or transit cards!

Simply tap your card against the back of your phone, and PembacaKUE will instantly display your complete card information in seconds.

💳 **SUPPORTED CARDS:**
• Mandiri e-Money / e-Toll
• BCA Flazz (Gen 2)
• BNI TapCash
• BRI BRIZZI
• KMT (Kartu Multi Trip) Commuter Line
• JakCard Bank DKI

🚀 **KEY FEATURES:**
• **Instant Balance Check:** Tap your card and view your remaining balance in real-time.
• **Transaction History:** Track your recent spending and top-ups (exclusive to cards that support history storage).
• **Voice Announcer:** Driving or in a rush? PembacaKUE can read your balance out loud automatically! Choose from a variety of unique voice packs.
• **Modern Cosmic UI:** Features a sleek, high-contrast dark mode interface that is comfortable on the eyes, whether you're using it at night or under bright sunlight.
• **Card Number Copying:** Easily copy your card number to your clipboard for quick top-ups on mobile banking or e-commerce apps.

🔒 **SECURE & PRIVACY-FOCUSED:**
This app acts strictly as a "Passive Reader". PembacaKUE **DOES NOT** store, deduct balances, or alter any data inside your card. The reading process happens entirely locally on your device via NFC technology, requiring no logins or banking access whatsoever.

*(Note: This application only works on Android devices equipped with an NFC sensor).*
